# Lean Canvases - Tennis Suite

## Segment 1: Recreational Players & Amateur Competitors
**Problem:** Hard to find evenly matched opponents; unorganized local tournaments; frequent disputes over scores or line calls.
**Solution:** LFG Drafter for matchmaking, deterministic scoring engine, verifiable dispute resolution protocol.
**Unique Value Proposition (UVP):** A unified, transparent ecosystem for matchmaking, verified scoring, and progression tracking.
**Unfair Advantage:** AI-assisted matchmaking, anti-smurfing (Outlier Detection), and automated dispute resolution.
**Customer Segments:** Club players, local league participants, casual weekend players. **Early Adopters:** Tech-savvy competitive amateurs seeking structured play outside pro circuits.
**Channels:** Social media groups, local club partnerships, grassroots tournament sponsorships.
**Key Metrics:** Active matches played per week, user retention rate, successful match completions without disputes.
**Cost Structure:** Hosting, development, community marketing.
**Revenue Streams:** Freemium model, tournament entry fees, cosmetic/progression unlockables.

## Segment 2: Club Owners & Facility Managers
**Problem:** Unused courts during off-peak hours; administrative overhead for leagues/tournaments; revenue leakage from manual booking systems.
**Solution:** Automated Scheduler engine, Staff Scheduler for umpires/ball-kids, integrated Finance ledger.
**Unique Value Proposition (UVP):** Zero-overhead facility management and automated tournament organization that maximizes court utility.
**Unfair Advantage:** Complete vertical integration from court booking to player XP, reducing reliance on third-party POS systems.
**Customer Segments:** Private tennis clubs, municipal facilities. **Early Adopters:** Boutique, modern tennis clubs looking to digitize operations.
**Channels:** B2B direct sales, industry conferences, trade publications, direct outreach to facility directors.
**Key Metrics:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), court utilization rate, churn rate among partnered facilities.
**Cost Structure:** Server/Database costs, B2B sales team, dedicated customer support.
**Revenue Streams:** SaaS subscription tiers, transaction fees on bookings and tournament registrations.

## Segment 3: Event Organizers & Broadcasters
**Problem:** Lack of professional broadcasting tools for amateur/mid-tier tournaments; manual scorekeeping is prone to errors; low audience engagement.
**Solution:** Bracket generation engine, Broadcaster UI with sub-200ms SSE telemetry, Cinematic AI commentary.
**Unique Value Proposition (UVP):** Broadcast-quality overlays and automated tournament bracket management accessible to grassroots tournaments.
**Unfair Advantage:** Sub-200ms real-time telemetry combined with AI automation to instantly generate highlight clips.
**Customer Segments:** Tournament directors, local sports broadcasters, amateur streaming groups. **Early Adopters:** Independent tournament organizers and niche tennis content creators.
**Channels:** Event sponsorships, partnerships with regional tennis associations, content creator outreach.
**Key Metrics:** Tournaments hosted, stream concurrent viewers, social syndication reach.
**Cost Structure:** High-bandwidth infrastructure for SSE, AI API compute costs.
**Revenue Streams:** Premium event licensing, white-label broadcasting solutions.
