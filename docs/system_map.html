System Access & Role Branches

Walled Garden Architecture & Middleware Flow for Tennis Suite

ADMIN / HOST

Path: /admin
  • Visibility: Complete system access.
  • Capabilities: Create/publish tournaments, manage teams, override live match status, and access raw telemetry.
  • Restrictions: None.

REFEREE

Path: /referee
  • Visibility: Active matches they are assigned to officiate.
  • Capabilities: Live score mutation, offline queue syncing, marking warmups and completions.
  • Restrictions: Cannot modify rules or see other referees' matches.

BROADCASTER

Path: /broadcast
  • Visibility: Real-time read-only view of active match states.
  • Capabilities: Subscribes to Server-Sent Events (SSE) for overlays.
  • Restrictions: Blocked from modifying any match states.

PLAYER

Path: /team
  • Visibility: Their team's schedule and head-to-head records.
  • Capabilities: Agent OS (AI Chat) and upcoming match previews.
  • Restrictions: Blocked from seeing private strategies of others or mutating scores.

MARSHALL

Path: /tournaments
  • Visibility: Master view of all courts and matches.
  • Capabilities: Dispute resolution and court assignments.
  • Restrictions: Cannot act as a referee or directly mutate scores.
